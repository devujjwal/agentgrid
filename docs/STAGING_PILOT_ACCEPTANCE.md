# Four-resource staging pilot

This is a prepared noindex staging catalogue, not approval to launch the canonical
production domain. The unchanged launch gate requires at least 36 prepared
resources, at least three useful resources in every category, distinct intents
and related links within the complete approved inventory. Four resources do not
pass that gate. Production credentials, domain/auth configuration, retention and
recovery operations and full real community acceptance remain unfinished.

## Real pilot sequence

1. Review and authorize the separately prepared named-account staging access.
   No additional staff authority or global registration is included.
2. Deploy the exact validated main commit to the existing staging destination.
   Verify its provider identity, capture provenance and delivery boundaries.
3. Have the consenting tester complete real sign-in. Inspect only the named
   verified managed identity, then apply the reviewed expiring database admission.
   The tester must choose their profile and explicitly accept current terms and
   privacy information themselves. Do not pre-create consent or attribution.
4. Test the real member's saved list, private draft, fork lineage, immutable
   submission and version history. Check owner isolation from both accounts.
5. Record a genuine pilot review/report with its testing context, then exercise
   applicable moderation and creator feedback paths. Preserve conflict-of-interest
   denials rather than granting extra authority to get around them. Never invent
   a testimonial, popularity count or successful outcome.
6. Verify session renewal, concurrent tabs, sign-out, failed/uncertain writes,
   responsive and keyboard behavior, resource withdrawal/recovery and attribution
   privacy. Record observed outcomes and unresolved failures separately.
7. Retire the additional HTTP pilot entry after acceptance. Database admission
   expiry prevents later onboarding but does not delete an established member or
   their immutable records. Shared Auth and PicMagic resources are never cleanup
   targets. Owned-data retention remains a separate launch requirement.

One live evidence write was refused by request verification. Readback confirmed
that no observation had been recorded; refreshing the page and restoring the
retained input allowed a single successful save. This demonstrates that recovery
path, not a diagnosed cause or complete session/concurrency acceptance.
